# Remote Work Policy

Document ID: DOC-002  
Policy owner: People Operations  
Effective date: March 1, 2026  
Status: Active  
Supersedes: DOC-005

## Purpose

This policy defines when Northstar Meridian Group employees may work remotely and
which approvals are required.

## Eligibility

Regular full-time and part-time employees may request remote work when their job
responsibilities can be performed securely away from an office.

Remote work is not automatically available for every position. The employee's manager
and People Operations must confirm that the role is suitable.

## Working from Another State

An employee must obtain written approval before regularly working from a state other
than their assigned work state.

People Operations will coordinate any required tax, payroll, employment-law, benefits,
and information-security review. Employees may not begin working from the new state
until approval is complete.

Temporary travel does not automatically change an employee's assigned work state, but
employees should contact People Operations when work from another state will exceed
ten business days.

## International Remote Work

Working outside the United States is prohibited unless Legal, People Operations, Tax,
and Information Security provide written approval.

## Security Requirements

Remote employees must use company-managed devices, multifactor authentication, and
the approved virtual private network when accessing restricted systems.

Confidential information must not be discussed or displayed where unauthorized people
can see or hear it.
