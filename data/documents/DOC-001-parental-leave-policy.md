# Parental Leave Policy

Document ID: DOC-001  
Policy owner: People Operations  
Effective date: January 1, 2026  
Status: Active

## Purpose

This policy explains the paid parental leave available to eligible employees of
Northstar Works, a fictional organization used only for this portfolio project.

## Eligibility

A regular full-time employee becomes eligible after completing 90 calendar days of
employment. Contractors, interns, and temporary workers are not eligible for paid
parental leave under this policy.

## Paid Parental Leave

An eligible employee may receive up to 12 weeks of paid parental leave following the
birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.

The leave must be taken within 12 months of the qualifying event. It may be taken as
one continuous period or, with written approval from People Operations, in blocks of
at least one full workweek.

## Notice and Documentation

When the need for leave is foreseeable, the employee should notify their manager and
People Operations at least 30 calendar days before the planned leave begins.

The employee must provide reasonable documentation confirming the birth, adoption,
or foster placement. Medical details beyond what is necessary to administer leave
must not be requested.

## Pay and Benefits

Paid parental leave is paid at 100 percent of the employee's regular base salary.
Health-plan participation continues during approved leave, subject to the employee's
normal contribution requirements.

Bonuses, commissions, overtime, and other variable compensation are not included when
calculating parental-leave pay.

## Return to Work

An employee returning from approved parental leave will ordinarily return to the same
position or a substantially equivalent position, subject to applicable law.

Questions about this synthetic policy should be directed to the fictional People
