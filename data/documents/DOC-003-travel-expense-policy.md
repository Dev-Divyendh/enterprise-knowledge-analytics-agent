# Travel and Expense Policy

Document ID: DOC-003  
Policy owner: Finance  
Effective date: February 1, 2026  
Status: Active

## Purpose

This policy defines reimbursable business-travel expenses and required supporting
documentation.

## Booking Requirements

Employees should use the approved corporate booking service when reasonably available.
Air travel should use economy class unless Finance approves an exception before
booking.

## Receipt Requirements

Expenses of 25 USD or more require an itemized receipt.

For lodging reimbursement, the employee must provide an itemized hotel folio showing
room charges, taxes, and additional services. A credit-card statement showing only the
total charge is not sufficient.

## Reimbursement Limits

| Expense category | Standard limit | Required evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Hotel | 225 USD per night | Itemized hotel folio |
| Meals in standard-cost cities | 75 USD per day | Itemized receipts when an individual expense is 25 USD or more |
| Meals in high-cost cities | 100 USD per day | Itemized receipts when an individual expense is 25 USD or more |
| Ground transportation | Reasonable actual cost | Itemized receipt when available |

The daily meal limit is the maximum total for the day. It is not a separate allowance
for each meal.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Personal entertainment, minibar purchases, traffic fines, upgrades without approval,
and expenses for family members are not reimbursable.

## Submission Deadline

Expense reports should be submitted within 30 calendar days after travel ends.
