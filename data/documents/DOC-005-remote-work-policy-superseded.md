# Remote Work Policy — Superseded Version

Document ID: DOC-005  
Policy owner: People Operations  
Effective date: June 1, 2024  
Status: Superseded  
Replaced by: DOC-002

## Authority Notice

This policy is retained only for historical reference. It was replaced by DOC-002 on
March 1, 2026 and must not be used as the current remote-work policy.

When this document conflicts with DOC-002, the active DOC-002 policy takes precedence.

## Previous Approval Rule

Under this former policy, an employee could work from another state after receiving
approval from their direct manager.

The former policy did not expressly require the additional People Operations, payroll,
tax, legal, and security reviews now required by DOC-002.

## Historical Security Rule

Employees were required to use a company-managed device and the approved virtual
private network when working remotely.

## Superseded Status

These rules are not current. Questions about present remote-work requirements must be
answered from DOC-002 while identifying this document as superseded when a conflict is
relevant.
